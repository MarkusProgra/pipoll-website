FAQ

Common questions

What is the human economy?

The human economy is a new way of thinking about personal development. Instead of just tracking habits, you become a "stock" whose value rises as you build consistency. Others can see your progress, and you can invest in them too.

How does my stock value work?

Your stock value starts at an IPO price and increases based on habit completion and streak maintenance. The more consistent you are, the more your value grows — just like a real stock.

Is Pipoll free to use?

Yes! Pipoll is free to download and use. You can track habits, build streaks, and watch your value grow without any payment required.

How many habits can I track?

You can track up to 4 custom habits at a time. We believe in focused, meaningful habit building rather than overwhelming yourself with too many goals.

What happens if I break a streak?

Your stock value may decrease, but you can always rebuild. The market forgives — just like life. What matters is getting back on track.

Can I see other users' progress?

Yes! The social market lets you browse other users' stocks, see their value trends, and become their investor. It's accountability meets community.
